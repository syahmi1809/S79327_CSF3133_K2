About the event

Welcome to the event.This event is designed to help you solve common coding errors.

Keynote Speaker: Dr Amir Ngah

Dr Amir Ngah is a renowned computer scientist with expertise in programming languages and software development. He will share his insights on effective coding practices and error resolution techniques.

Guest Speaker: Dr Mashita

Dr Mashita is a respected researcher in the field of software engineering. She will discuss strategies for debugging and improving code quality.
